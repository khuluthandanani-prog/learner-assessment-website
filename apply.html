Learner application

On a phone you can take the photo with the camera or choose one from your gallery. Show the parent's face clearly. Only RISE COLLECTIVE staff can see this photo. It is never shown to learners.

The parent logs in with the learner's student number and this password. A parent can look at the learner's subjects, notes, videos, assessments, marks and rankings, but cannot change anything.

By applying you agree that RISE COLLECTIVE may keep these details, documents and the parent selfie for the application and may contact the parent on WhatsApp, including about new notes, videos and assessments in the chosen subjects.

Junior tutor application

Write each school name in full, the same way the learners write it on their applications. Learners will only see the notes, videos and assessments of the tutor who teaches at their school.

By applying you agree that RISE COLLECTIVE may keep these details and documents for the application.
